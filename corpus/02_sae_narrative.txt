Serious Adverse Event Narrative - Study NX-301 (protocol amendment 4, approved 2025-11-14)

Subject NX301-0007, a 91-year-old woman from Rangeley, ME 04970, was admitted to hospital on March 3, 2026 with grade 3 hepatotoxicity (ALT 8x ULN). Her daughter, Ruth Marsh, reported jaundice two days earlier. Prior work-up excluded Wilson's disease (ATP7B wild-type). Principal Investigator Dr. Priya Raman assessed the event as possibly related to study drug. A liver panel specimen labeled EM-19340822 was sent to the central lab, and PK sample NX301-0007-PK03 was collected on 2026-03-04. The subject was discharged on March 9, 2026.
